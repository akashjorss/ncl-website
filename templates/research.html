What we’re exploring

Our near-term focus is industrial observability: inferring unobserved temperature, flow, and other physical fields from sparse measurements. Thermal systems, cooling infrastructure, and fluid processes offer concrete places to test these ideas.

We investigate diffusion models, neural operators, continuous representations, and physics-informed learning. The architecture is an open question. Experiments, benchmarks, and reproducible software help us decide what works.

NCL draws on research in neural 3D reconstruction and generative models at LISN, Université Paris-Saclay, and is part of the Inria Startup Studio ecosystem.
