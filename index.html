Physics and AI, applied to industry

Most of the physical world is never measured.

Neural Continuity Labs builds physics-grounded AI that reconstructs what the sensors miss: the state of an industrial system everywhere, not only where it is measured, with uncertainty an engineer can sign off on.

Talk to us How it works

The real world, unlike the simulated one, is rich and noisy. It is not a spherical cow. We build for that world: every model we train is a hypothesis about a physical system, and every deployment is a test of it. We keep what survives.

Approach

01

Start from the equations

The physics of a plant is known: conservation, transport, dynamics. We build it into the model instead of hoping a network rediscovers it from data.

02

Finish with the data

Measurements carry what no equation does: wear, drift, the habits of this particular machine. The model learns the distance between the textbook system and the real one.

03

Say how sure you are

Every estimate comes with its uncertainty. An engineer should know when to trust the model and when to walk over and look.

About

Neural Continuity Labs grew out of doctoral research in AI and 3D reconstruction at Université Paris-Saclay.

It is founded by Akash Malhotra and supported by Inria Startup Studio.

The name is the research program behind the company: Neural Continuity, the study of differentiable computation as a regime with a boundary.
